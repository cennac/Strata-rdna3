# CPU pool workers 12 vs 9 on the adopted profile: 9 workers are slower once the RDNA read is on

b478 paired the [adopted profile](2026-11-24-v0220-grdna-adoption.md), with the RDNA hyper-connection read in
both arms, against the same profile with `--pool-workers 9`, on dual RX 7900 XTX (26/22 split, ROCm 10.1,
Qwen3.8-Flash-Next GSQ-RCO IQ3_XXS). Same integrated 0.1.66 binary from `53b8629`, environment, expert-profile seed
and cache budgets; the worker count is the only difference. The engine binds the first nine workers to logical CPUs
1-9 beside the host thread on 0; further workers run unpinned on the remaining logical CPUs.

## Quality

The two arms are token-identical at all 26 frozen probes with identical forced rollbacks; both keep the adopted
profile's reference shape (the 7 raw Chinese greedy probes differ from the older reference, sampled and code probes
match). The objective suite showed no new failures and the scored-text degeneration checks passed.

## Paired result

Three alternating fresh-load pairs; pair 2 reversed the workload order. All 36 scored requests
finished at 1,024 output tokens with caches off.

| Workload | 12 workers tok/s | 9 workers tok/s | Throughput | TTFT | Faster pairs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Chinese single | 127.20 | 123.80 | -2.67% | +0.30% | 0/3 |
| Chinese dual | 178.54 | 172.02 | -3.65% | -0.02% | 0/3 |
| Code single | 166.20 | 166.30 | +0.06% | -0.80% | 2/3 |
| Code dual | 241.59 | 235.40 | -2.56% | -0.35% | 1/3 |

Not adopted: every lane lost, and three of them by more than 2%. The earlier [9-worker gain](2026-11-23-v0220-pool9.md) was measured without the RDNA read; with the read on, the GPU side of a window is shorter and the CPU expert supply matters more, so the three extra workers help. The profile keeps 12 workers. Production restoration was verified after the window; no deployment or `main` change. Per-pair values:
