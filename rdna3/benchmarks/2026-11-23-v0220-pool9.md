# CPU pool workers 12 vs 9 on the exact + EXPDEC profile

b475 paired the [b472 profile](2026-11-23-v0220-expdec.md) with `--pool-workers 12` against the same profile with `--pool-workers 9`, on dual RX 7900 XTX (26/22 layer split, ROCm 10.1, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next GSQ-RCO IQ3_XXS). Same integrated 0.1.66 binary from `53b8629`, same environment, expert-profile seed and cache budgets; the worker count is the only difference. The host has 10 physical cores. With 12 workers the engine warns about oversubscription and leaves three workers unpinned; with 9 it binds one worker per core 1–9 beside the host thread on core 0, without the warning.

## Quality

Both arms matched all 14 raw and 12 sampled probes of the frozen [0.1.65 reference](../reference-0.1.65.json) with forced rollbacks 35/21, at identical validation residency (12,905 / 9,623 slots). Graph and penalty-path checks passed; the objective gate showed no new failures.

## Paired result

Three alternating fresh-load pairs; pair 2 reversed the workload order. All 36 scored requests finished at 1,024 output tokens with caches off.

| Workload | 12 workers tok/s | 9 workers tok/s | Throughput | TTFT | Faster pairs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Chinese single | 116.20 | 119.00 | +2.41% | +0.11% | 2/3 |
| Chinese dual | 160.24 | 164.54 | +2.69% | -0.22% | 3/3 |
| Code single | 158.80 | 160.00 | +0.76% | +1.04% | 2/3 |
| Code dual | 229.37 | 225.39 | -1.73% | -0.82% | 2/3 |

Chinese dual improved in all three pairs by 2.69% and meets the adoption rule; no workload lost more than 2% of median throughput and TTFT moved by at most 1.0%. The gain is not uniform: code dual's median fell 1.73% (it was faster in two pairs and slower in one), so the worker count trades a little code-dual throughput for about 2.5% on the Chinese lanes. Treat 9 as the opt-in choice when Chinese lanes matter most; 12 remains better for code dual.
