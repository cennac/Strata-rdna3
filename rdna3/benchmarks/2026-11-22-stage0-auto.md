**Mechanism.** Putting the chain on the front card makes stage-0's window stream contend with it — the configuration the engine's own comment warns about ("the chain shares its card with … the next launch on stage 0 waits for it", mtp.cpp). Stage 1 waits on stage 0's hand-off, so the solo pipeline's throughput is gated by the front card, and the front card's windows got 1–2.8 ms slower. The bottleneck card's +517 expert slots (CUDA1 9623→10140) help the dual lanes' per-window work more than they can help a pipeline whose front half is now starved. The dual-lane gains are real and consistent with b421's raised-priority pattern (batch lanes gain from CUDA1 relief), but the predeclared rule protects every lane, and the primary solo lane collapsed.
